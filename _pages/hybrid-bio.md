# About

I completed my PhD in Computer Science at Western University in 2026, supervised by Professor Nazim H. Madhavji. My dissertation, *AI and Blockchain-oriented Technology for Analysing Security Issues in CI/CD Logs*, studied security-relevant operational data, explanation and evidence integrity.

My work includes DevSecLogs, a research framework and analyst-facing prototype for stage-aware CI/CD security evidence. I have undertaken research collaboration through the IBM Centre for Advanced Studies and taught computing courses in Canada and Bangladesh.

## Education

- PhD in Computer Science, Western University (2026)
- MSc in Computer Science, American International University-Bangladesh (2015)
- BSc in Computer Science, American International University-Bangladesh (2013)

## Research approach

I combine systematic reviews, empirical investigation, software prototypes and traceable security evidence. Anomaly scores are investigation signals, not definitive security conclusions.
